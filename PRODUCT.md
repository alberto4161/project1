# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Plain static HTML/CSS (user choice, no build step).

## Users
Two audiences on one site: recruiters/employers evaluating the owner personally, and prospective clients or partners for a business opportunity. The owner's background, per the supplied photos, is soccer event and media work (Gold Cup, Leagues Cup, USMNT match-day access, press-room settings) and playing soccer.

## Product Purpose
A personal website that doubles as the front door for a business opportunity. Success means a visitor understands who the owner is and takes a next step (contact, hire, or engage).

## Positioning
Not yet decided. Open decision.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The business offering is undecided and must not be invented in copy.
- Personal site comes first; the business section is a placeholder until defined.

## Evidence on Hand
- 10 photos in `images/` (stadium and match-day credentials, press-room desks, headshot, playing action).
- No testimonials, client names, metrics, pricing, or press. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Lead with real, first-hand evidence (the photos), not generic claims.
- One site, two audiences: personal credibility first, business offer second.
- Leave undecided facts visibly open rather than inventing them.
